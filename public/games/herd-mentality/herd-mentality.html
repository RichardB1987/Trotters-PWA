How to play

  • Read the question. Everyone secretly writes an answer on their phone, then shows it.
  • Tap the top half of a player's cow for +1, the bottom half for −1.
  • Score 1 point if your answer is in the biggest group. If two or more groups tie for biggest, everyone in each tied group gets +1.
  • Anyone left on their own (not in a group) takes the Pink Cow – drag it to them.
  • First to 8 points wins – but not while holding the Pink Cow!
  • Made a mistake? Tap Undo.
